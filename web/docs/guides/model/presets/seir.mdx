# SEIR

<EndpointSelector />

The Susceptible-Exposed-Infected-Recovered model. Extends SIR with an exposed (latent) period before individuals become infectious.

## Compartments

- **Susceptible**: individuals who can be infected
- **Exposed**: individuals who are infected but not yet infectious (latent period)
- **Infected**: individuals who are currently infectious
- **Recovered**: individuals who have recovered and are immune

## Transitions

- **Susceptible &rarr; Exposed**: mediated by `transmission_rate` and the number of Infected individuals
- **Exposed &rarr; Infected**: spontaneous at `incubation_rate`
- **Infected &rarr; Recovered**: spontaneous at `recovery_rate`

## Default parameters

| Parameter | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `transmission_rate` | 0.3 | Rate at which susceptible individuals become exposed through contact with infectious individuals |
| `incubation_rate` | 0.2 | Rate of progression from exposed to infectious (1 / incubation period in days) |
| `recovery_rate` | 0.1 | Rate at which infected individuals recover (1 / infectious period in days) |
